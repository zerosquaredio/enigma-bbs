# CONNECT.ANS

The connect screen callers see for a moment before the login matrix, on
any theme that has no `CONNECT` art of its own (ENiGMA½'s stock theme has
none). It is also the splash screen a board offers its Cyberspace hub by
default, since the board finds it first (`atProto.publishing.splash.art`).

A synthwave sunset: a striped sun on the horizon, a sky shaded from black
through blue to a cyan haze, a magenta grid floor. 80x24, 16 colours.

By Nexus Lounge (cyberspace), 2026, made for the Cyberspace Framework,
under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): share and
adapt it, crediting "Nexus Lounge". Because it is free to share, a board
that keeps it may let its hub show it with no question of whose art it is.
